Last updated: July 3, 2026

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the Digital Receipt Wallet app:

  1. Open the app and log in
  2. Go to the Settings screen
  3. Tap "Delete Account" at the bottom
  4. Confirm the deletion in the dialog that appears

What Happens After Deletion

Your account is deactivated immediately. It remains in this state for 30 days, during which you can log back in to reactivate it. After 30 days, your account and all associated data (including receipts, transactions, and profile information) are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

If You No Longer Have Access to the App

If you've uninstalled the app or can't access your account, you can request account deletion by emailing us at mobinovax@gmail.com. Including the email address associated with your account will help us process your request faster.
